Studio Reservations & Arrival Protocols

To preserve serenity and allow thorough sanitization of each Canadian cedar cabin and private suite between guest visits, we operate under strict scheduling protocols:

Arrival Window (10–15 Mins Prior)

Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to enjoy our herbal hydration bar, change at an unhurried pace, and review your therapy settings.

Late Arrival Policy

If you arrive late, your session must still conclude at the pre-scheduled time so we can accommodate the subsequent guest without disruption. The full session fee will apply.

Guest & Companion Passes

Our larger multi-occupancy cabins can comfortably host companions. Companion add-ons must be reserved prior to the session and all companions must execute an individual health waiver.

Hydration & Towel Protocol

Clean organic bamboo and cotton towels are provided. Guests must sit on a towel inside cabins at all times to protect untreated cedar timber and maintain hygiene standards.

Health Clearance, Medical Disclaimer & Assumption of Risk

Vital Medical Warning: Infrared sauna thermal therapy produces core body heating, elevated heart rate, and heavy perspiration similar to moderate cardiovascular aerobic exercise. It is essential that you review contraindications carefully.

Medical Disclaimer: Services, therapies, literature, and consultations provided at Aura & Solace are intended solely for general relaxation, personal comfort, and wellness enhancement. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any illness, disease, or medical condition.

Absolute & Relative Contraindications:

You agree that you will NOT utilize our infrared saunas without explicit prior written clearance from your licensed physician if you have any of the following conditions:

  • • Pregnancy or suspected pregnancy (core hyperthermia is contraindicated for fetal health).
  • • Cardiovascular implants, pacemakers, defibrillators, or uncontrolled coronary artery disease.
  • • Systematic Lupus Erythematosus or Multiple Sclerosis (heat sensitivity exacerbation).
  • • Hemophilia or active acute hemorrhage disorders.
  • • Current intoxication, influence of narcotics, barbiturates, or alcohol.
Voluntary Assumption of Risk: By utilizing studio facilities, you acknowledge that you have voluntarily chosen to enter an environment involving radiant heat and optical photobiomodulation. You knowingly assume all risks of personal discomfort, lightheadedness, dehydration, or heat exhaustion resulting from your own failure to hydrate or heed therapist guidance.

Sanctuary Etiquette & Guest Conduct

Aura & Solace is an oasis of silence, restorative grounding, and sensory reset. We hold all members and guests to high standards of considerate conduct:

Quiet & Digital Silence
Mobile devices must be placed on silent mode. Phone calls are strictly prohibited in treatment suites, corridors, and relaxation lounges.
Zero Tolerance Substances
Smoking, vaping, illicit substances, and alcoholic beverages are strictly forbidden everywhere on the studio premises.
Cleanliness & Timber Care
No body lotions, heavy essential oils, or bronzers before entering saunas, as they stain untreated Canadian hemlock and cedarwood.
Staff & Guest Respect
Any harassment, inappropriate behavior, or verbal abuse towards sanctuary staff or fellow guests will result in immediate termination of membership.
