Visitor Protocol

Preparing for Your Session

To ensure you receive the deepest relaxation and maximize the restorative effects of your session, we ask clients to follow these straightforward preparation steps:

Hydrate in Advance

Drink 500ml–750ml of water or electrolyte-rich liquid throughout the 2 hours before your appointment. Avoid heavy meals 60 minutes prior.

Arrive 10 Minutes Early

This allows time for peaceful check-in, water service, and personal gowning in your private suite without cutting into your sauna time.

What to Wear

Clean swimwear, workout attire, or simple towel wrap. For Red Light sessions, exposed clean skin absorbs the most photon energy.

Post-Session Cool Down

We provide private rainfall showers, non-toxic shampoo and body wash, chilled towels, and electrolyte tonics to replenish trace minerals.

Therapy Questions Answered

Most clients experience noticeable relaxation and sleep benefits with 2 to 3 sessions per week. Beginners often start with 1 session per week at a moderate temperature (50°C) before gradually increasing duration or frequency.

We encourage complete digital disconnection. While phones can be kept in the outer suite locker, electronic devices should not be brought inside the heated cedar cabin, as heat can trigger thermal battery protection shutdowns.

While 660nm and 850nm light does not contain harmful UV rays, the medical LEDs are quite bright. We provide protective blackout goggles in each suite for comfort while facing the panels directly.

Infrared heat is not advised for pregnant individuals, those with acute fever, severe hypotension, or unmanaged cardiovascular conditions. If you have an internal cardiac pacemaker or take prescription vasodilators, consult your doctor.
