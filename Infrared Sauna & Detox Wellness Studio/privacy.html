Sanctuary Commitment

Commitment to Your Privacy & Dignity

At Aura & Solace Infrared Wellness Sanctuary ("Aura & Solace", "we", "us", or "our"), we provide an atmosphere of restorative peace, sensory calm, and deep physiological rejuvenation. We believe your digital and medical privacy deserves the exact same standard of uncompromising care and integrity.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, safeguard, and respect your personal information, booking reservations, transaction records, and sensitive health intake assessments when you visit our website, schedule an appointment, create a client account, or experience services at our physical studio located in Westlake Village, California.

1

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as data automatically generated through your digital interactions with our booking system and web platform:

Contact & Identification Data

Full name, primary email address, mobile phone number, physical mailing address, and emergency contact details requested upon studio registration.

Booking & Account Credentials

Secure password hashes, appointment dates, therapist preferences, session durations, private cabin selections, and guest notes.

Billing & Transaction Details

Payment transaction records, billing addresses, and membership subscriptions. Credit card data is tokenized directly via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (Stripe/Square); we never store raw credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers.

Device & Telemetry Data

IP address, browser type, operating system version, page response latency, and interface preferences (such as Dark/Light mode and LTR/RTL reading direction).

2

Health & Wellness Intake Information

Special Health Data Safeguards: Because thermal therapies and medical-grade photobiomodulation create cardiovascular and cellular responses, guest safety requires a confidential pre-session health screening.

Prior to your initial appointment, you are invited to complete an intake form covering relevant wellness factors, including:

  • Cardiovascular Health: Pacemakers, defibrillators, hypertension, hypotension, or cardiac conditions contraindicated for elevated thermal exposure.
  • Pregnancy Status: Infrared saunas and prolonged core thermal elevation are strictly contraindicated during pregnancy.
  • Implants & Surgeries: Joint replacements, silicone implants, or recent acute surgical operations.
  • Prescription Medications: Use of diuretics, barbiturates, blood thinners, or medications that impair the body's natural heat regulation or photosensitivity.
  • Sensory Preferences: Sound frequencies, chromotherapy light wavelengths (e.g. 660nm Red vs 850nm Near-Infrared), and cabin temperature target ranges (120°F – 158°F).

Confidentiality Guarantee: Health intake responses are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and restricted solely to licensed wellness directors and certified studio therapists to ensure your session is administered safely. They are never sold, rented, or shared with commercial entities.

3

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses small text files called cookies and local storage tokens to ensure flawless navigation, secure client authentication, and personalized viewing preferences:

Cookie / Token Category Purpose & Operation Duration & Management
Strictly Essential Maintains active client sign-in sessions, authenticates booking requests, and secures CSRF tokens. Session-based; cleared upon logout or browser close.
Preference & Accessibility Remembers your Dark/Light theme mode (solace_theme) and RTL reading layout direction. Persistent (12 months); saved in your local browser storage.
Performance & Analytics Anonymously monitors page load speeds, error rates, and popular wellness articles to optimize site usability. Persistent (30 days); strictly aggregated without personal identifiers.

Your Cookie Control: You can configure your browser (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge) to notify you before accepting cookies or block cookies altogether. Please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent login or booking checkout features from operating correctly.

4

How We Use Your Information

We process personal information only for legitimate studio operational purposes, client safety, and contractual fulfillment:

  • Appointment Execution: Reserving private cedar sauna suites, assigning certified practitioners, and calibrating infrared heating chambers prior to your arrival.
  • Health Risk Mitigation: Reviewing medical contraindications to protect your cardiovascular wellness and physiological safety during intense heat sessions.
  • Transactional Communication: Sending automated email and SMS confirmations, calendar invites, reschedule alerts, and billing receipts.
  • Client Membership Services: Tracking monthly credit balances, guest passes, and rollover allowances within your client dashboard.
  • Studio Communications (Optional): Delivering our curated Wellness Journal featuring evidence-conscious health articles, detox guidance, and private member invitations. You may opt out anytime via the one-click unsubscribe link.
5

Data Security & Storage Protocols

We implement industry-leading technical and administrative defenses to ensure your personal and health records are shielded from unauthorized access, accidental destruction, or illicit disclosure:

In-Transit Encryption
All digital communications between your device and our sanctuary are secured via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with SHA-256 SSL certificates.
At-Rest Encryption
Client records and intake questionnaires are encrypted with AES-256 hardware encryption in SOC 2 Type II compliant enterprise cloud centers.
Role-Based Access
Strict principle-of-least-privilege access; front desk team members cannot access sensitive intake responses without supervisory authorization.
Routine Auditing
Quarterly internal security vulnerability evaluations, staff privacy training, and automated data integrity verification routines.
6

Third-Party Services & Disclosures

We partner with vetted, professional third-party services exclusively to power studio operations. All third-party providers are contractually bound to maintain rigorous confidentiality and are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose:

  • Payment Processors: Stripe & Square for PCI-compliant financial transaction settlement.
  • Booking & Scheduling Engine: Cloud-based reservation systems for calendar availability and studio cabin allocation.
  • Notification Gateways: Twilio / SendGrid for delivering automated appointment reminders and verification PINs.
  • Cloud Hosting & CDN: High-availability secure infrastructure with automatic DDoS mitigation.
Zero Data Selling Policy: Aura & Solace has never sold, rented, or traded client data to third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or marketing brokers, and will never do so in the future.
7

Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and governing regulations (including the California Consumer Privacy Act “CCPA”, California Privacy Rights Act “CPRA”, and General Data Protection Regulation “GDPR”), you possess enforceable legal rights regarding your personal records:

Right to Access & Portability

Request a complete copy of the personal and session data we maintain about you in a machine-readable format.

Right to Rectification

Request instant correction of outdated contact information, emergency phone numbers, or health intake amendments.

Right to Erasure (“To Be Forgotten”)

Request permanent deletion of your profile, session notes, and account data, subject to tax and legal compliance retention duties.

Right to Opt-Out & Non-Discrimination

Withdraw consent for non-essential communications at any time without experiencing price discrimination or degraded studio service.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, email our designated privacy team at privacy@auraandsolace.com with the subject line “Privacy Rights Request”. We verify identity and respond within thirty (30) calendar days at zero fee to you.

8

Data Retention Protocols

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Active Member Profiles: Maintained continuously throughout your active membership and for twenty-four (24) months after your last studio visit to preserve session preferences and rollover benefits.
  • Financial & Transaction Ledger: Retained for seven (7) years in accordance with federal and California state commercial tax accounting statutes.
  • Intake Questionnaires: Updated annually upon renewal, or securely destroyed upon verified account closure request.
9

Children's Privacy Protection

Aura & Solace thermal wellness facilities and digital booking interfaces are designed exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. Guests aged 16 and 17 may access sauna suites only with the explicit in-person written consent and accompaniment of a parent or legal guardian.

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal records from children under the age of 16. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 16 without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to permanently delete the data from our databases.
