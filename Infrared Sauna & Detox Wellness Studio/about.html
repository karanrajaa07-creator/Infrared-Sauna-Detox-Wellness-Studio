Origin & Philosophy

A Respite From Constant Acceleration

In modern urban life, our nervous systems rarely drop out of high-alert fight-or-flight mode. Constant blue light from screens, persistent mental fatigue, and sedentary work leave both mind and body depleted.

Aura & Solace was founded in 2021 as a sensory sanctuary. We selected sustainably harvested Canadian Western Red Cedar cabins for their antimicrobial phytoncides, outfitted every room with medical-grade ultra-low EMF heaters, and designed private suites so our clients can experience complete digital disconnection in peaceful privacy.

"We believe true self-care is not a luxury indulgence, but an essential maintenance ritual for human vitality, restorative sleep, and nervous system resilience."

— Elena Vance, Founder & Lead Director
