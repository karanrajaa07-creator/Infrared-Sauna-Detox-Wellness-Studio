Infrared Sauna Sessions

Traditional steam saunas heat the ambient air up to 85°C–95°C (185°F–205°F), creating a heavy, hot atmosphere that can be uncomfortable to breathe. In contrast, full-spectrum infrared saunas operate at gentle, comfortable temperatures (45°C–60°C / 115°F–140°F). The infrared wavelengths directly penetrate muscle and tissue, stimulating natural core sweating without taxing your respiratory system.

Full-spectrum encompasses Near Infrared (NIR), Mid Infrared (MIR), and Far Infrared (FIR) wavelengths. Near infrared supports skin surface vitality; mid infrared penetrates into soft tissue to promote circulation and ease stiffness; and far infrared stimulates the core thermal receptors to trigger restorative perspiration.

Preparation & Etiquette

We supply plush organic cotton bath sheets, hand towels, chilled eucalyptus compresses, rainfall shower toiletries, and alkalized filtered electrolyte water. You may wear swimwear, light workout clothing, or wrap in our provided linens. Comfortable, loose-fitting attire is recommended for afterward.

We recommend arriving 10–15 minutes prior to your scheduled time. This allows you to check in, complete your digital health check, enjoy complimentary herbal tea, and step into your pre-warmed suite without rush.

Drink at least 500ml (16–20 oz) of water 1 to 2 hours before your session. Avoid alcohol or heavy meals right before thermal therapy. After sweating, replenish electrolytes using our chilled mineral infusion at the concierge lounge.

Health, Safety & Contraindications

Infrared saunas are generally very gentle, but thermal heat is contraindicated for pregnant individuals, individuals with acute systemic fever, uncontrolled hypertension, or decompensated congestive heart failure. If you have an internal cardiac pacemaker or take heat-sensitive prescription medications, consult your physician prior to booking.
