Common Inquiries

Everything You Need to Know Before Your Visit

Curious about what to wear, how our temperatures work, or how to prepare? Here are our most frequent visitor questions.

View All FAQs

Each session takes place inside a 100% private sanctuary suite. Most clients prefer to wear a swimsuit or simply wrap themselves in the provided organic cotton towels. Clean cotton robes and slippers are provided in your suite.

While traditional saunas reach 80°C–95°C (180°F–200°F), our infrared saunas operate at a much more comfortable 45°C–60°C (115°F–140°F). Because infrared light warms your core rather than suffocating the air, you can breathe comfortably while achieving a deeper sweat.

Red Light Therapy utilizes specific narrow wavelengths (660nm visible red and 850nm near-infrared). It contains zero UV radiation, produces virtually no heat, and will not cause tanning or sunburn. It safely stimulates cellular energy and collagen.

Appointments can be easily rescheduled or cancelled directly through your Client Dashboard up to 6 hours before your appointment time at zero charge. Unused session credits are instantly returned to your balance.
